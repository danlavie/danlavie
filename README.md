## About Me

I'm **Dan Lavie**, an independent SEO consultant, WordPress developer, and digital technology specialist based in Israel.

With **20+ years of experience** in web development, search engine optimization, digital marketing, and online business solutions, I help businesses improve their visibility, performance, security, and digital infrastructure.

My work combines traditional **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)** with emerging approaches to **AI-powered search and discovery**, including Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), AI Optimization (AIO), and Search Experience Optimization (SXO).

Unlike traditional SEO consulting, my approach goes beyond keywords and content.

I work directly with the underlying technology - from website architecture, structured data, and technical SEO to server infrastructure, performance engineering, security, and automation.

**My philosophy:** Effective search optimization starts with a technically sound, fast, accessible, and maintainable website.
